## Working agreement

- Use one main group chat (for example WhatsApp or Teams) and one shared repository/folder.
- Hold regular meetings according to availability; record decisions, task owners and deadlines in writing.
- Respond within 24 hours on normal project days and faster near submission. Notify the team early about absence, blockers or delays.
- Follow shared component/naming conventions and a consistent report style. Share and test code regularly.
- Discuss tensions respectfully using task evidence, agree revised deadlines or support, reassign urgent tasks when necessary, and record changes. Escalate unresolved problems to the tutor through the spokesperson.
- Aim to finish at least 24 hours before the Canvas deadline. The documents supplied here do not provide that deadline.

## Delivery stages

1. Planning: finalize sitemap, data model, named role allocation and repository access.
2. Prototype: inspect and understand the Vue/Vite routes, Bootstrap layout, sample data and authentication screens.
3. Build review: verify search/sort/pagination, details, validation, role permissions, database persistence, CRUD and social actions.
4. Advanced work: each member should agree and document their own Vue-related learning and implementation; demonstrate the relevant feature.
5. Review: test mobile/tablet/desktop, keyboard operation and accessibility; capture report screenshots and record actual findings.
6. Final: deploy to Mercury, rehearse and record the continuous video, review files and submit before the real Canvas deadline.

| Item                     | Owner and constraint                                                                                                                                                                    |

The application and group report marks are adjusted by peer evaluation, and the final mark is adjusted by interview performance. The brief totals 40 marks: functionality 5, technical requirements 5, advanced features 10, group report 5, individual report 5, interview 10.

These reports, video, peer evaluations, actual role assignments and university submission have not been completed by creating the application.
